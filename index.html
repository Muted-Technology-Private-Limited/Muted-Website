Muted Aero One vs Conventional HEPA

Two different approaches to particulate air purification.

MUTED AERO ONE

Electro Settling Technology

Muted Aero One uses our Electro Settling Technology to capture airborne particles on washable collection plates, reducing dependence on disposable particulate filters.

  • ✓Electro Settling Technology
  • ✓Filterless particulate collection
  • ✓Washable collection plates
  • ✓Reusable particle collection
  • ✓No routine disposable filter replacement
  • ✓Reduced disposable filter waste
  • ✓Easy collection-plate cleaning and reuse
  • ✓Designed for lower recurring maintenance burden
Airborne particles → Electro Settling → Washable plates
VS

CONVENTIONAL HEPA PURIFIER

HEPA Filter Technology

Conventional air purifiers use replaceable HEPA filters to capture airborne particles. These filters require periodic replacement as they load with captured particles.

  • ✓HEPA filtration technology
  • ✓High particulate filtration efficiency
  • ✓Established filtration approach
  • ✓Replaceable filter system
  • ✓Widely available technology
  • ✓Straightforward filter replacement
  • ✕Electro Settling Technology
  • ✕Filterless particulate collection
  • ✕Washable collection plates
  • ✕Reusable particle collection
  • ✕Filter-replacement-free operation
Airborne particles → HEPA filter → Periodic replacement
